I am a motivated Computer Science graduate with hands-on experience across programming, software development, and cloud computing. My practice blends technical precision with problem-solving — the kind of work that makes me a useful addition to any team trying to build something durable.

My goal is to continually grow in the IT sector, developing depth in software engineering, cloud solutions, and emerging technologies like AI. I treat every project as a chance to refine craft, not just ship features.

At Providence Software Solutions, I'm a Junior Software Developer working full-stack with C#, ASP.NET, React, and SAP API integrations — designing responsive interfaces, improving system reliability, and streamlining enterprise processes. I'm also a Certified IBM AI Assistant Engineer, building and deploying AI-driven agents using IBM watsonx and Microsoft Copilot Studio.

Before that, I tutored first and second-year students at IIE Varsity College in C# and Java, helped run a family fragrance business and rebuilt its e-commerce platform, and handled large-volume data capture at a car rental firm — each role teaching me a different rhythm of work.

When I'm not coding or studying the latest in software and cloud, I'm reading about how good systems are designed and trying to apply that thinking everywhere — from a button's hover state to a deployment pipeline.
